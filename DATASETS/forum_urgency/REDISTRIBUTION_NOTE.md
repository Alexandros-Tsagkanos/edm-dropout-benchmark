# Redistribution note (read before publishing this folder)

The files in this folder are third-party data, included here so the semester
package is runnable for grading:

- `All_Courses_REDACTED_CODED.csv` and the accompanying `README.md` /
  `LICENSE` come from the supplementary repository of Švábenský et al.
  (EDM 2023). The MIT `LICENSE` covers their code, per their README; the
  coded CSV is their own redacted release.
- `Stanford.csv` is their redacted derivative of the Stanford MOOC Posts
  dataset (Agrawal & Paepcke 2014), redistributed publicly in their
  repository. The original Stanford dataset has its own access terms.

For a public paper artifact or repository release, do not re-redistribute
`Stanford.csv` from here: link to the Švábenský et al. repository (or to the
original Stanford source) instead, exactly as `DATASETS/stanford_mooc_posts/provenance_stanford.md` does for the original tarball
(URL + SHA-256, no bundling).
