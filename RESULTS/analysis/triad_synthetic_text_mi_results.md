# TRIAD-Drop's GPT comments are near-perfectly label-predictive (synthetic-text ceiling)

Reproduce: `python CODE/analysis/triad_synthetic_text_mi.py`
(reads `dataset_with_comments.csv` from `TRIAD Zerkouk Github.zip` via `zipfile`; writes
`triad_synthetic_text_mi_results.csv`). Encoder, pooling, and leakage-safe steps are imported from the
pipeline's own `phase9_multimodal_bert.encode_comments` + `common` — identical protocol to Phase 9.

## Setup
TRIAD-Drop augments the UCI Realinho dataset (the same tabular base we use) with a
`student_comment` column generated by GPT-4.5 from each student's attributes (see
`../../DATASETS/triad_drop/provenance_triad.md`). We run only the text through our leakage-safe protocol (frozen DistilBERT
→ train-only PCA(32)+scaler → classifier) and predict dropout (`y = 1[Target=="Dropout"]`, n=4,423,
dropout rate 0.321 — same as Realinho).

## Result — text alone almost perfectly recovers the label

| Text-only model | AUC | F1 (dropout) | Accuracy |
|---|---|---|---|
| Logistic Regression | 0.994 | 0.936 | 0.958 |
| Random Forest | 0.988 | 0.900 | 0.938 |

Rough MI estimate (kNN, for context only): summed Î(text-PCA32; y) ≈ 0.97 bits, i.e. essentially
the entire label entropy (H(y) ≈ 0.91 bits).

## Why this matters (apples-to-apples, same base)

| Quantity (Realinho base) | F1 | Note |
|---|---|---|
| TRIAD GPT text, text-only | 0.94 (AUC 0.99) | this analysis (our split/protocol) |
| TRIAD reported multimodal | 0.88 (binary; abstract: "89% accuracy, F1 0.88") | their split — not directly comparable to ours; their repo README's 0.85 is a three-class macro-F1 |
| Tabular baseline (our P2) | ~0.79 (AUC ~0.92) | our committed numbers |
| Our templated text, text-only | 0.70 (I(text;y)=0.28 bits, 31% of H(y)) | `simulated_text_mi_results.md` |

The GPT comments are so strongly label-correlated that the text alone reaches AUC 0.99 — above even
the tabular AUC (~0.92), which means the comments carry label information beyond the student
attributes. The leak is even visible lexically: 89.9% of Graduate-row comments literally contain
"graduat" (vs 0.7% of Dropout rows, 7.1% of Enrolled), and 49.3% of Dropout-row comments contain
"drop"; a three-line keyword rule ('graduat'→Graduate, 'drop'→Dropout, else Enrolled) already attains
77% three-class accuracy with no model at all. The "multimodal gain" is therefore largely a
recovery of injected label signal, not evidence of a real text effect. (We compare leakage-safe
protocols on their released comments, not test-set scores — their 0.88 is on their own split.)
Crucially, TRIAD does not disclose the text as synthetic-induced or quantify this, whereas we
(a) deliberately inject less signal (0.28 bits) with noisy templates, (b) disclose it as a
ceiling, and (c) quantify it.

Protocol note: `encode_comments` is imported from phase 9 unchanged, including its 48-token
truncation window — the GPT comments are short, so truncation discards little, but we disclose it.
